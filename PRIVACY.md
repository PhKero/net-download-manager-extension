# Privacy Policy — Net Download Manager Browser Integration

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

This policy covers the **Net Download Manager Browser Integration** add-on for Firefox, Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. It works together with the free Net Download Manager desktop app for Windows (https://github.com/PhKero/net-download-manager).

## Summary
The add-on does **not** send any data to us or to any server. It only talks to the Net Download Manager app installed on **your own computer**. We have no servers, accounts, analytics or advertising.

## What the add-on reads, and why
To do its one job, sending downloads from the browser to the desktop app, the add-on reads:

| Data | Why it is used | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Website content**: links, video and audio addresses on the page you are viewing | To show the "Download this video" button and the "Download all links" list | Only to the desktop app on your computer (localhost) |
| **Web history**: the address and title of the current tab | To send the page or its video to the app and name the file | Only to the desktop app on your computer |
| **User activity**: your clicks on the add-on's buttons and menus, and downloads the browser starts | To know what you asked to download and hand browser downloads to the app | Only to the desktop app on your computer |
| **Cookies** of the site you download from | So downloads that need your login on that site work | Only to the desktop app on your computer |

The add-on does not read anything in the background. It only reads these things when the page loads its download button or when you choose to download.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect, store or send this data to the developer or to any third party.
- We do not sell or transfer user data.
- We do not use data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending.
- The add-on does not load code from the internet.

## Data stored on your computer
- The add-on keeps its own settings in the browser's local storage, for example which sites are excluded.
- The desktop app keeps your download list on your computer.
- You can remove both by uninstalling the add-on and the app.
